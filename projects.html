Archer vs Assassin — AI Battle Training System

A Unity 2D symmetric combat training system where 100 AI units battle on a 120×80 battlefield. Powered by genetic algorithms and neural networks (48→48→7), each generation selects the best-performing archer and assassin, clones their brains, and mutates them for the next round.

Highlights: team vision sharing, cover-based tactics, ammo logistics (10 arrows + 4 supply points), focus-fire coordination, assassin bait mechanics, exploration grid with fog-of-war, anti-regression best-ever tracking, and atomic JSON save/load with crash recovery.

Shogun AI — Game Auto-Play System

A Python-based AI that automatically plays Shogun Showdown — a turn-based tactical roguelike. The pipeline captures the game screen, parses the state with OpenCV pixel analysis, then makes tactical decisions via a multi-layer rule engine or local LLM (Ollama).

Highlights: threat-response priority system, lethal combo calculator (1/2/3-skill combos), smart positioning with Wanderer swap passive, stuck detection with auto-recovery, a playbook system that records successful tactics and replays them in similar situations, and multi-backend support (rule engine / Ollama / Claude API).

Old Shanghai — Artwork

This artwork captures the nostalgic charm of the Bund in old Shanghai, featuring its iconic clock tower, classic architecture, and the famous red trolleybuses. Using a vintage color palette of warm grays and earthy tones, contrasted by the bright red of the bus and fresh green trees, this painting brings the history and daily life of the past to the canvas. It took me a month to paint and was honored as an "Outstanding Work" in the People's Art Youth Creation Activity.
