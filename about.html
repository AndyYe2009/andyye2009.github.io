About Me

My background, skills, and journey so far

Bio

I am a Grade 12 student at Shanghai Datong High School's International Division, driven by a passion for combining technology and art. I believe in learning through practice, and my curiosity always leads me to create new things.

My interest in artificial intelligence inspired me to participate in the Youth AI Camp: Future Leaders in AI Entrepreneurship, where I learned to use AI tools to build intelligent systems. This experience led to the development of several projects. I created Formula 0.5, an AI racing agent trained to drive through various F1 tracks at top speeds. I also built Archer vs Assassin, a Unity-based AI battle simulation where units evolve combat tactics using genetic algorithms and neural networks, and Shogun AI, a Python-based system that uses computer vision to automatically play a strategy game.

Along with my technical work, I have spent years improving my art skills in painting and comic, earning a Comic Level 8 certification and multiple gold awards including the MeiYuan Cup and the International Children's Art Competition.

Beyond my individual projects, I have actively built my leadership and teamwork skills. Serving as Vice President of the Voice Acting Club and as a member of the Student Union's Activities Department has helped me develop my ability to communicate, negotiate, and organize complex events, from club training sessions to large-scale school festivals. I also participated in the Friendship Ambassador Training Camp and the UCLA Game Lab Summer Program, broadening my global perspective.

I hold two invention patents: an innovative scooter design and a water surface floating object collection boat. My early passion for engineering earned me first prize in the National Robotics Competition (Building Category).

I am a dedicated learner committed to exploring new ideas where science, technology, and creativity meet.
