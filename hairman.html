Hairman — AI Hairstyle Recommendation

A Youth AI Camp team concept for an AI hairstyle recommendation app

During a Youth AI Camp, I led a 2-person team to create Hairman, an AI-powered hairstyle recommendation web concept. Our playful slogan was: "With great hairstyles comes a better mood"—designed to save users from unfortunate haircuts before the "evil haircut" ruins everything.

My Role & Vision

Users could upload a photo to have the app identify their face shape and preview matching hairstyles. As lead programmer, I built the prototype and pitched it to an investor evaluation panel. We originally envisioned publishing it as a full mobile app on app stores.

Expectation vs. Reality

While it remained at the prototype and pitch stage rather than a production-ready app, this project was my first taste of translating a creative concept into a working demo, bridging technical prototyping with real-world product presentation.
