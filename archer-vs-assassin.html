50 archers vs 50 assassins. Each generation picks the best fighter, clones its brain to the whole class, and mutates. Over hundreds of generations, the units evolve real tactics — cover, focus-fire, even baiting.

🧠 Neural Network — 48 inputs

A 48 → 48 → 7 feedforward network with Tanh activation. The 7 outputs drive movement, aiming, attacking, and two abilities.

  • Self state (6): HP/class signal, cooldowns, nearest wall, distance from center, facing
  • Opponent state (9): relative position, distance, HP, facing, cooldowns, skill active
  • Projectile awareness (15): 3 projectiles × 5 dims (position, velocity, friend/foe)
  • Team density (6): allied/enemy counts at Near/Mid/Far ranges
  • Nearest ally (3), local advantage (1), ammo/home distance (1)
  • Nearest cover (3), opponent-behind-cover raycast (1), ammo urgency + supply (2), ability-2 cooldown (1)
🏹🗡️ Class Design

Archer — HP 1, ranged arrow (0.5s cd), Q dodge (3s), W charged shot (7s, 2 arrows), 10 arrows (+3/kill, 4 supply points).

Assassin — HP 2, melee dagger (0.8s, hits all in range), Q parry arrows in a 45° arc (1.5s), W dash slash (3.5s).

🧬 Genetic Evolution
  • Per-generation best archer and assassin selected, brain cloned to all 50 units, then mutated
  • Anti-regression: best-ever brain preserved and restored if a generation regresses
  • Mutation rate 0.15, strength 0.3 · auto-save every 5 generations (atomic JSON, max 3 files)
👁️ Team Vision & Fog of War
  • Archers see 25 units, assassins 12; cover blocks line-of-sight via raycast
  • Teammates share vision — if any ally sees an enemy, the whole team does
  • Per-team exploration grid (12×8 cells) decays after 5s; assassins march to unexplored cells
🤝 Team Coordination
  • Grouping bonus near 2+ allies; isolation penalty when alone and surrounded
  • Archer–assassin escort bonuses; focus fire (3+ archers on one target); bait mechanic
🛡️ Cover System
  • 20 random cover objects (blue blocks), dual colliders (solid + arrow-blocking trigger)
  • Cover-to-cover advance, ambush-from-cover bonus, penalty for hugging obstacles
⚖️ Scoring & Rewards
  • Hit +10, kill +25, get hit −3, death −5, escalating time penalty, 120s force-death timeout
🛡️ Safety & Performance
  • Training mode skips effects; static sprite caching; 1000 projectile cap + 3s cleanup
  • Null-reference guards, 2GB memory watchdog, atomic save with corrupt-file quarantine
