Overview

Archer vs Assassin is a Unity 2D AI combat simulation where 100 AI units (50 archers + 50 assassins) battle on a 120×80 battlefield. Each generation, the best-performing archer and assassin are selected — their neural network brains are cloned to all 50 units of the same class, with small mutations applied to introduce variation. Over hundreds of generations, the AI evolves sophisticated combat tactics.

Neural Network Architecture

Each unit is controlled by a 48 → 48 → 7 fully-connected feedforward neural network with Tanh activation. The 7 outputs control movement, aiming, attacking, and two abilities.

48 Input Dimensions

  • Self state (6): HP/class signal, attack cooldown, skill cooldown, nearest wall distance, distance from center, facing opponent
  • Opponent state (9): relative position, distance, HP, facing me?, cooldowns, skill active, distance from wall
  • Projectile awareness (15): 3 projectiles × 5 dimensions (position, velocity, friend/foe)
  • Team density (6): allied/enemy counts at Near/Mid/Far range brackets
  • Nearest ally (3): relative position, facing direction
  • Local advantage ratio (1)
  • Ammo / distance from home (1): arrows remaining (archer) or distance from spawn (assassin)
  • Nearest cover (3): distance, direction
  • Opponent behind cover (1): Raycast detection
  • Ammo urgency + supply distance (2): archer-specific
  • Ability 2 cooldown (1)

Class Design

🏹 Archer

  • HP: 1 | Speed: 6.0 | Vision: 25 units
  • Attack: Ranged arrow, 1 damage, 0.5s cooldown
  • Q - Dodge: Dash 4.5 units away from danger, invulnerable during animation (3s cooldown)
  • W - Charged Shot: 2 damage, cannot be parried, 7s cooldown, costs 2 arrows
  • Ammo system: Start with 10 arrows, -1 per shot, +3 per kill, 4 supply points on map
  • Shoot discipline: no shooting beyond 20 units, selective at 10-16, free fire under 10

🗡️ Assassin

  • HP: 2 | Speed: 7.5 | Vision: 12 units
  • Attack: Melee dagger, 1 damage, 0.8s cooldown, hits all enemies in range
  • Q - Parry: Deflect arrows in a 45° arc (1.5s cooldown), rewards +3 score per deflection
  • W - Dash Slash: Charge 3 units forward at high speed, damaging enemies on contact (3.5s cooldown)

Key Systems

Genetic Evolution

  • Selection: Per-generation best archer and best assassin (regardless of team)
  • Elite cloning: Best brain → all 50 units of same class → mutate each copy
  • Anti-regression: Historical best brain preserved; if generation performance drops, revert to best-ever
  • Mutation: rate=0.15, strength=0.3
  • Auto-save: Every 5 generations, JSON atomic write, max 3 files retained

Team Vision & Fog of War

  • Archers see 25 units, assassins see 12 units
  • Cover blocks line-of-sight via Raycast
  • Teammates share vision — any ally sees enemy → whole team sees enemy
  • Per-team exploration grid: 12×8 = 96 cells, decays after 5 seconds
  • Assassins without enemies march toward nearest unexplored cell

Team Coordination

  • Grouping bonus: +0.01/step when near 2+ allies
  • Isolation penalty: -0.03/step when alone and surrounded
  • Archer-Assassin escort: archers +0.03/step when assassin within 8 units; assassins +0.02/step when archer within 10 units
  • Focus fire: 3+ archers hitting same target → commander bonus +5
  • Bait mechanic: assassin draws enemy fire while archer kills → assassin +8 bonus

Cover System

  • 20 randomly placed cover objects (2-4 unit size, blue blocks)
  • Dual colliders: solid (blocks characters) + trigger (blocks arrows)
  • Assassin cover tactics: shelter reward, cover-to-cover advance bonus, ambush from cover +5
  • Cover collision penalty: -0.05/step for hugging obstacles

Scoring & Rewards

  • Hit enemy: +10
  • Kill enemy: +25 (attacker)
  • Getting hit: -3
  • Death: -5
  • Time penalty: escalating (-0.005 → -0.01/step after 90s)
  • 120s force-death timeout to prevent endless matches

Safety & Performance

  • GPU: Training mode skips flash/trail/slash effects, only renders characters and cover
  • Memory: Static sprite caching, observation array reuse, 1000 projectile cap + 3s cleanup
  • Crash protection: Full null-reference guards, 120s timeout, 2GB memory watchdog, 9999 gen cap
  • Save system: Atomic write (tmp → rename), corrupt file quarantine, backward compatible
