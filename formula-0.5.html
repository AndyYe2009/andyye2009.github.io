Formula 0.5 — AI Racing

A Unity experiment where AI cars learn to drive a track

After building Archer vs. Assassin, I wanted to tackle continuous vehicle dynamics. I created Formula 0.5 in Unity, where 20 AI cars learn to drive on procedurally generated tracks.

Technical Highlights

  • Custom Neural Network: Built without external libraries (20 inputs → 16 hidden → 2 outputs for steering and throttle).
  • Raycast Sensors: Each car uses 8 raycast sensors covering a 150° field of view to sense track boundaries.
  • Genetic Algorithm: Implemented evolutionary training with anti-degradation, cloning and mutating top-performing "brains" each generation, complete with JSON checkpointing.

Some of the code

Expectation vs. Reality

While the cars successfully learned track-following behavior, they still struggled with collision avoidance and perfect racing lines. Though a training demo rather than a full game, it successfully validated my end-to-end neural network and evolutionary algorithm pipeline.
