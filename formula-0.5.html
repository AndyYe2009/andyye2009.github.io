Every generation, the fastest car's brain is cloned to all 20 cars with small mutations. Over hundreds of generations, they learn to hug the perfect racing line — no ML-Agents, just a hand-written network.

🧠 Neural Network (hand-written)

A 20 → 16 → 2 feedforward network with Tanh activation. Two outputs: throttle and steering. Built from scratch in C#, including mutation, cloning, and JSON serialization.

👁️ Perception — 20 inputs
  • 8 raycast sensors over a 150° field of view (distance to walls/obstacles)
  • Speed, angular velocity, velocity alignment, throttle, distance from track center
  • Circuit progress and track-direction alignment
🧬 Genetic Evolution
  • Fastest car per generation becomes the parent; brain cloned + mutated (rate 0.15, strength 0.3)
  • Anti-regression best-ever brain; 3× time scale, 30 FPS cap, 180-second races
🛣️ Procedural Track Generation

Ellipse + dual-frequency wave perturbations guarantee a closed, non-self-intersecting loop. Auto-builds the road, curb strips, 8 checkpoints, and start line — a unique track every run.

🎯 Reward Engineering
  • Forward bonus, wrong-way penalty, curb slowdown (teaches the optimal line)
  • Progress-scaled rewards, lap +500, crash −100, idle penalty
💾 Save & Safety
  • Atomic JSON save/load, NaN/Infinity validation with corrupt-save quarantine, rolling 3 saves
