Overview

Formula 0.5 is a Unity 2D AI racing simulator where 20 self-driving cars compete on a procedurally generated track. Each car is driven by a hand-written neural network (built from scratch — no ML-Agents or external AI frameworks), and the population evolves through a genetic algorithm: every generation, the fastest car's brain is cloned to all 20 cars with small mutations. Over hundreds of generations, the cars learn to find the optimal racing line and complete laps at top speed.

Neural Network Architecture

Each car is controlled by a 20 → 16 → 2 fully-connected feedforward neural network with Tanh activation. The two outputs control throttle and steering. The network is implemented entirely from scratch in C#, including feedforward, mutation, cloning, and JSON serialization.

Perception System

The 20 input dimensions describe the car's view of the world:

  • 8 raycast sensors spanning a 150° field of view with 6-unit range (distance to the nearest wall or obstacle)
  • Speed (normalized) and angular velocity
  • Velocity alignment — how closely the car's movement matches its facing
  • Current throttle
  • Distance from track center (normalized to half track width)
  • Circuit progress — how far around the track the car has traveled
  • Track direction alignment — whether the car faces forward along the track

Genetic Evolution

  • Selection: The car with the highest fitness score per generation becomes the parent
  • Elite cloning: The best brain is cloned to all 20 cars, then each copy is mutated
  • Mutation: rate = 0.15, strength = 0.3
  • Anti-regression: The best-ever brain is preserved; if a new generation performs worse, it reverts
  • Fast training: 3× time scale, 30 FPS cap, 180-second races

Procedural Track Generation

The track is generated procedurally from an ellipse combined with dual-frequency wave perturbations, which mathematically guarantees a closed loop with no self-intersection. The system automatically builds the road mesh, glowing curb strips, 8 checkpoints, a start line, and collision walls. This means every training run can use a unique, valid circuit.

Reward Engineering

The fitness function is the heart of the system — it teaches the AI the correct racing behavior:

  • Forward bonus for driving along the track direction
  • Wrong-way penalty for driving backward
  • Curb slowdown — driving on the track edges cuts speed, teaching the AI the optimal racing line
  • Progress-scaled rewards — speed and distance rewards grow as the car advances around the circuit
  • Lap completion: +500
  • Crash penalty: -100 (plus off-track stuck detection after 2 seconds)
  • Idle penalty for staying still

Checkpoint & Lap System

  • 8 checkpoints are placed around the track
  • Each car must pass them in order; wrong-way detection resets progress and applies a heavy penalty
  • Completing a full lap records the lap time and awards a large fitness bonus

Save & Safety

  • Atomic JSON save/load (write to a temp file, then rename)
  • NaN/Infinity validation on every saved brain — corrupt saves are quarantined
  • Best-ever brain preserved across sessions, with a max of 3 rolling save files
