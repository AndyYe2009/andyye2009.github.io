Formula 0.5 — AI Racing

A Unity experiment where AI cars learn to drive a track

Built with Claude. I set the direction, but Claude helped me write and debug most of the C#. This is a learning project, not something I built from scratch alone.

A Unity simulation where 20 AI cars learn to drive around a procedurally generated track. Each car runs a small neural network I wrote in C# (20 inputs → 16 hidden → 2 outputs: throttle and steering). Every generation, the fastest car's brain is copied to all 20 cars with small mutations, so over many generations they get better at getting around the track.

What's actually in it

  • A hand-written neural network (20 → 16 → 2) with no external AI libraries
  • 8 ray sensors per car, spread over a 150° field of view
  • A genetic algorithm with anti-regression (it keeps the best-ever brain)
  • A track that's generated fresh each run (an ellipse with wavy edges)
  • JSON saving every 5 generations

What I wanted vs. what actually happened

The cars do learn to follow the track, but they still crash a lot, and the result is not a clean, perfect racing line. It's a training demo, not a finished game — there's no menu or real gameplay, just the simulation running. What I actually got out of it was watching a neural network and a genetic algorithm work end to end, which was the point.
