UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute

Three games in one summer — design, 3D, and 2D

During the UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute, I completed four core courses: Game Programming, Graphic Design, Game Design, and Audio Design. Below are three game projects I created during the program:

Lethal Spire — Card-Based Dungeon Crawler

I led a 4-person team to design this card game, serving as team lead and taking charge of mechanics design, rulebook writing, and balancing. To subvert conventional gameplay, we designed the player as a demon boss who must strategically help four heroes defeat him. Players combine room-specific attributes with card mechanics to shield heroes from hazards, utilizing various items like Excaliburs, wands, and even beer.

Surviving Light — 3D Zombie Exploration Prototype

Developed independently under professor guidance, this 3D prototype focuses on environmental exploration. While my original vision included chasing mechanics, time constraints led me to streamline the core objective around collecting light orbs across custom-carved terrains with a portal system.

Protato — 2D Survival Game

In this 2D survival game, players embody the last remaining zombie hunted by humans. Designed around subverting player expectations, the game features a deceptive portal labeled "A wall is lying" that leads to an instant "game over," teaching players through trial and error that the true exit is hidden behind destructible walls.
