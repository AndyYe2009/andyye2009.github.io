UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute

Three games in one summer — design, 3D, and 2D

July 2025 at UCLA. I built three projects — led a card game as team leader, then made a 3D and a 2D game on my own. All three share a theme I clearly enjoy: playing the bad guy.

🃏 Lethal Spire — card game (Team Leader)

I led a 4-person team to design a Gothic dark-fairytale card game where you play the villain boss defending your lair. I wrote the rules, balanced the mechanics, and directed the art.

🧟 3D — Zombie Survival Prototype

A solo 3D survival prototype: collect four experience orbs while zombies roam. It was an exercise in guided level design — steering the player with visual hints instead of text. I made a custom sky shader, sculpted terrain, and built a working portal system.

🥔 Protato — 2D Survival

You're the last zombie on Earth, hunted by humans. The heart of it is a fake portal that leads to an instant bad ending ("A wall is lying.") and a real exit hidden behind a breakable wall — teaching the player through failure.
