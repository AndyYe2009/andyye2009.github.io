UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute

Three games I made myself — no AI help

In July 2025 I went to the UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute and made three games. Unlike my AI projects, these I built myself — I'm saying that clearly because it matters to me that the distinction is honest.

1. Lethal Spire — a card game (team leader)

I led a 4-person team to design a card game where you play the villain defending your lair. I wrote the rules and balanced the mechanics.

2. A 3D zombie survival prototype

A solo 3D prototype: collect four orbs while zombies roam. I built a simple sky shader, terrain, and a portal system.

3. Protato — a 2D survival game

A 2D game where you're the last zombie, hunted by humans. The main idea was a fake portal that leads to a bad ending, with the real exit hidden behind a breakable wall.
