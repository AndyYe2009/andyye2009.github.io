UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute

Three games in one summer — design, 3D, and 2D

In July 2025 I went to the UCLA Game Lab Summer Institute and made three games — I led a card game as team leader, then made a 3D and a 2D game on my own. Unlike my AI projects, these I built myself. All three share a theme I enjoy: playing the bad guy.

1. Lethal Spire — a card game

A Gothic dark-fairytale card game where you play the villain boss defending your lair against a band of heroes. We worked as a 4-person team and divided up the work — everyone had their own part to handle. My part was leading the team, writing the rules, balancing the card mechanics, and directing the art. 2. A 3D zombie survival prototype

A solo 3D prototype. The goal is simple: collect four experience orbs while zombies roam the level. I guided the player through the game using text and collectibles. I built a custom sky shader, sculpted the terrain, and set up a working portal system.

3. Protato — a 2D survival game

A 2D game where you're the last zombie on Earth, hunted by humans. The main idea is a fake portal that leads to an instant bad ending — a sign reads "A wall is lying" — while the real exit is hidden behind a breakable wall. The game teaches the player through failure. I built this one on my own.

I originally wanted to make this a roguelike game, but with the time I had and my own technical limits, I could only make it into what it is now.
