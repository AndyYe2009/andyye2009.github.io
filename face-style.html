Face Style (焕颜) uploads a front-facing photo, identifies your face shape, and recommends hairstyles that actually fit — then previews them before you commit. I led a 3-person team to build the product and pitch it to shareholders.

🧠 AI Face-Shape Analysis

Deep-learning face recognition classifies a front-facing photo into 6 face shapes — round, square, heart, diamond, oval, and oblong — each mapped to the styles that flatter it.

💇 Smart Hairstyle Matching

A multi-factor engine weighs face shape, style preference, hair texture, and life scene. Hairstyles span short (fade, crew cut, buzz), medium (wolf cut, mullet, textured perm), and long (center-part, man-bun, Japanese-style long).

👤 Virtual Try-On

AR real-time preview — see the hairstyle on your own photo before you commit, with zero trial-and-error cost.

📊 Personalized Style Report

Generates a personal image-analysis report and tracks your style evolution over time.

👥 Team & Roles

A 3-person team I led. I served as CTO — owning the AI vision model and the technical build — working alongside a CEO and a CSO.

📈 Business Plan

We built a full pitch for shareholders. Target: China's 300-billion-yuan beauty & hair market and 150M+ young users (18–35). Monetization: freemium, brand ads, e-commerce, and B2B salon tools.
