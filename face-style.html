Face Style (焕颜) — AI Hairstyle Recommendation

A summer-camp team pitch for an AI app idea

Built with Claude. This was a camp pitch and a prototype, not a real product. I want to be clear about that up front — an earlier version of this page over-stated what it was.

At a youth AI entrepreneurship camp in August 2026, I was part of a 3-person team that pitched an app idea: take a photo of your face, figure out your face shape, and suggest hairstyles that suit it. I was the CTO — I handled the technical side and the AI concept. We made a simple prototype and a pitch deck, and presented it to an investor panel as part of the camp.

What I actually did

  • Owned the AI concept: classify a face shape, then suggest matching hairstyles
  • Helped build a simple prototype — a demo where you place a hairstyle onto a photo
  • Presented the technical side to the panel

What it isn't

This never became a real app. There's no trained model with a real accuracy number — the "96% accuracy" and similar claims from an earlier version of this page were not true, and I've removed them. It was a camp project and a pitch exercise, and the value for me was learning how to take an idea to a demo and present it.
